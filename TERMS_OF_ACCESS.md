# Access and reuse conditions

Research code and fixed-model parameters for the AMI intestinal necrosis model are supplied as an encrypted archive. Downloading the archive does not grant a decryption password or permission to use or redistribute its contents.

The public documentation may be read and cited. Permission to access, execute, modify or redistribute the encrypted code and model contents must be established in the written terms supplied by the corresponding author. These instructions do not replace that agreement or grant rights beyond it.

- Use the decrypted materials only for the users and purposes expressly authorized in the written terms.
- Do not disclose decryption credentials or redistribute decrypted materials unless the written terms expressly permit it.
- Cite the associated article and identify the release version used in research reports, subject to the applicable agreement.
- Retain provenance when making authorized modifications. Do not describe a modified model or a changed input mapping as the unchanged published model.
- Do not place patient records, production databases, credentials or deployment secrets in the public repository.

The software and fixed model support research evaluation. Access does not authorize clinical deployment, establish diagnostic or treatment effectiveness, or substitute for the user's institutional research requirements. Prospective evaluation requires an appropriate study protocol and separate management of clinical care and research predictions.

Patient-level source data are excluded. Code/model access does not grant access to those data or guarantee exact reproduction of reported performance without the underlying study data. Synthetic examples are software tests only.
